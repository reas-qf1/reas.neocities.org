(last updated: July 1, 2026)

i started making music in 2017, when i was 11 years old, with a yamaha synthesizer that i was given for my music lessons and fl studio, later switching to fl mobile in 2018. at the time, music was just something i did for fun whenever i had free time from middle school, so i did not release much. also, i didn't care about quality control or anything, and i had no production experience, so all of the tracks from that time were garbage.

in 2019 i decided to pick up music as a hobby, splitting my at-the-time alias into several new ones and starting to release new music regularly in several different genres. along with discovering vaporwave/ambient at that time, and the covid lockdown giving me more free time, i was putting out a lot new music to a practically nonexistent audience.

in mid/late 2020, i started reaching out to labels. around that time, my music started getting a bit of traction. the pace continued on into 2021, with three full-length albums being released that year.

in 2022 i started getting into the glitchier style of vaporwave/plunderphonics, which led to the creation of the umbrella project equals0, which is mostly dedicated to messing with samples from various sources in various ways. most of 2022 was also dedicated to making my (as of now) magnum opus, Star Eclipse, which was released in 2023.

in 2023, as i entered uni leaving me not a lot of free time, and due to me becoming very perfectionist about my own music, i stopped releasing music regularly and started dedicating my time to learning the more technical aspect of music, such as mixing, mastering and sound design.

as of now, i've been working on an album for 2 years at this point, and i believe it's gonna be my next magnum opus. there are also several other things in the works in different stages of completion, as well as remasters of all my albums from Star Eclipse and earlier.
